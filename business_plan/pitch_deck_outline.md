# RegMap Pitch Deck Outline (angel meeting)

Eleven slides. Each slide lists the headline and the talking points. Keep one idea per slide and let the demo carry the weight.

## Slide 1: Title
- RegMap. Prove it once. Satisfy every framework.
- Founder name, contact, one line: compliance automation that maps your existing controls onto every framework you must satisfy.

## Slide 2: The problem
- Companies face more and more frameworks: SOC 2, then ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, CMMC.
- Proving that controls built for one satisfy another is done by hand, by expensive experts, and redone on every revision.
- This crosswalking step is slow, costly, fragile, and opaque.

## Slide 3: Why it matters now
- The number of frameworks keeps rising, and buyers now expect continuous compliance.
- Even well-funded compliance tools do not automate cross-framework mapping. That gap is the opportunity.

## Slide 4: The solution (and live demo)
- Map: a control goes in, ranked and scored target provisions come out, weak matches flagged as candidate gaps.
- Evidence: live findings become control-level evidence.
- Report: an auditable, exportable compliance package.
- Show the running product mapping a NIST control to the correct HIPAA provisions on screen.

## Slide 5: Proof it works
- Fine-tuning raises top-5 retrieval of the correct provision from 0.500 to 0.735 on held-out data, with confidence intervals, beating generic embeddings and keyword search.
- Three open-source models released, reproducible benchmarks, research to peer-review standard.

## Slide 6: Market
- Governance, risk, and compliance software is a large and growing market.
- Beachhead: United States mid-market companies pursuing a second or third framework.
- Expansion: every additional framework is more value per customer.

## Slide 7: Wedge and moat
- Wedge: automated cross-framework crosswalking, the part incumbents do worst.
- Moat: curated provenance-tagged mappings, proprietary ground truth and benchmarks, a data flywheel, and a training funnel.

## Slide 8: Business model and go-to-market
- SaaS tiers plus usage, sold to mid-market and to consultancies.
- Land on the wedge at the moment of pain, use the training funnel for low-cost acquisition.

## Slide 9: Team
- Founder who already built the platform, models, and research.
- Plus four hires: platform engineer, machine learning engineer, compliance expert, growth lead.
- Advisors to recruit in compliance and security go-to-market.

## Slide 10: Traction and roadmap
- Today: working platform, released models, benchmarks, research.
- Next 18 months: multi-tenant product, public-domain framework stack, three to five paid design partners, our own SOC 2, and a grant award.

## Slide 11: The ask
- Raising 750,000 on a SAFE, plus 275,000 to 400,000 in non-dilutive grants.
- Funds a five-person team for 16 to 18 months to first paid customers and a seed round.
- Close with the one line: your check is the catalyst that unlocks grants and first revenue.

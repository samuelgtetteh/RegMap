# RegMap (one-pager)

**Prove it once. Satisfy every framework.**

**What we do.** RegMap is compliance automation software that maps the security controls a company already operates onto every regulatory framework it must satisfy (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, CMMC and more), and keeps that mapping current. We automate regulatory crosswalking, the slow, expert-driven step that even well-funded compliance tools leave manual.

**The problem.** Every growing company eventually faces a second and third framework. Proving that controls built for one satisfy another is done by hand, costs tens of thousands in consulting time, and goes stale on every revision.

**Our wedge.** A fine-tuned model maps each control to the equivalent obligations in a target framework, returns a ranked and scored shortlist, and flags weak matches as candidate gaps. Automated, transparent, and audit-ready.

**Why us.**
- Working platform already built: a mapping engine served as an API, a console that exports audit-ready compliance packages, usage metering, and Kubernetes deployment.
- Three released open-source models and reproducible benchmarks: fine-tuning lifts top-5 retrieval of the correct provision from 0.500 to 0.735 with confidence intervals.
- Research to peer-review standard, which strengthens grants and buyer trust.

**Market.** Governance, risk, and compliance software is a large, fast-growing market. Our beachhead is United States mid-market companies pursuing their second or third framework, exactly where the manual pain appears.

**Model.** SaaS from about 12,000 to 40,000 per year plus usage, sold to mid-market companies and to compliance consultancies. About 82 percent gross margin.

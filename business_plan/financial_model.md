# RegMap Financial Model

All figures are forward-looking projections based on the assumptions stated here. They are estimates for planning and fundraising, not guarantees. Currency is United States dollars.

## 2. Revenue assumptions

- Pricing tiers: Starter near 12,000 per year, Continuous Compliance near 30,000 to 40,000 per year, plus a usage add-on.
- Blended annual contract value: 18,000 in Year 1, 22,000 in Year 2, 28,000 in Year 3, rising as the mix shifts toward the continuous tier.
- Paying customers at year end (cumulative): 6 in Year 1, 30 in Year 2, 85 in Year 3.
- Customers ramp through the year, so recognized revenue is lower than exit annual recurring revenue.
- Gross margin: about 82 percent, because the marginal cost of a mapping is a small amount of compute.

## 3. Three-year projection (summary)

| Metric | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 |

| Paying customers (year end) | 6 | 30 | 85 |

| Headcount (year end) | 5 | 9 | 15 |
| Operating expense | 840,000 | 1,500,000 | 2,900,000 |

Interpretation: Year 1 is a build-and-land year funded by the pre-seed and grants. A seed round in Year 2 funds the growth from five to fifteen people. By the end of Year 3 the business is approaching the efficiency where a Series A becomes the natural next step.

## 4. Unit economics (targets)

- Blended annual contract value maturing toward 28,000.
- Gross margin about 82 percent.
- Customer acquisition cost target of 6,000 to 10,000 blended, held down by the training funnel and a low-friction starter tier.
- Gross revenue retention target above 85 percent, with net retention above 100 percent as customers add frameworks.
- Lifetime value to acquisition cost ratio target above 3 to 1 by Year 3, with payback under 18 months.

## 5. The ask and the sources of capital

Primary structure: a **pre-seed SAFE of 750,000**, complemented by **275,000 to 400,000 of non-dilutive grants** (see grant strategy). Combined with early collected revenue, this funds the 18-month plan.

| Source | Amount | Timing |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Pre-seed SAFE (angels and pre-seed funds) | 750,000 | At first close |
| Non-dilutive grants (SBIR Phase I and state programs) | 350,000 | Months 4 to 12 |
| Early collected revenue | 100,000 | Months 8 to 18 |
| **Total sources over 18 months** | **1,200,000** | |

Eighteen-month cost of the five-person plan is about 1,256,000, so this is a lean, honest 16 to 18 month runway. The seed raise is expected to begin around month 12, well before cash runs low.

Alternative single-instrument structure for investors who prefer not to depend on grant timing: a **1,100,000 SAFE** that funds the full 18 months on its own, with grants as upside that extends runway rather than as a dependency.

## 7. Key assumptions and sensitivities

- The biggest driver of runway is the timing of the first grant award. If grants slip, the alternative 1,100,000 SAFE removes that dependency.
- The biggest driver of Year 2 revenue is the design-partner-to-paid conversion rate. We plan for a conservative six paying customers in Year 1 so the plan does not depend on a breakout.
- Salaries can flex down further (more equity, less cash) if the round comes in below target, extending runway.

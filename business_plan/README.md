Tagline: **Prove it once. Satisfy every framework.**

Stage: pre-seed / angel
Last updated: 2026-09-18

This folder is a complete, investor-ready package. Read in this order:

1. [executive_summary.md](executive_summary.md) is the two-page summary to send an angel before a meeting.
2. [business_plan.md](business_plan.md) is the full plan (problem, product, market, moat, GTM, team, risks).
3. [financial_model.md](financial_model.md) is the three-year projection, the five-person team budget, unit economics, and the use of funds.
4. [grant_and_funding_strategy.md](grant_and_funding_strategy.md) is the non-dilutive (grant) and dilutive (angel/seed) funding strategy, including the SBIR/STTR path.
5. [pitch_deck_outline.md](pitch_deck_outline.md) is a slide-by-slide narrative for the angel meeting.
6. [one_pager.md](one_pager.md) is a single-screen teaser for a first email or a warm intro.

## Honesty notes for the reader
- Traction claims in this plan refer only to assets that exist today: a working platform, three released open-source models, reproducible benchmarks, and research manuscripts. There are no customers or revenue yet; this is a pre-seed raise to reach first paid customers.
- All financial figures, market sizes, and timelines are forward-looking projections based on the assumptions stated inline. They are estimates, not guarantees.
- Style: no em-dashes anywhere, per the founder's standing rule.

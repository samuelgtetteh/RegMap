# RegMap: Executive Summary

**Prove it once. Satisfy every framework.**

## The one-sentence version
RegMap is compliance automation software that maps the security controls a company already operates onto every regulatory framework it must satisfy, turning a slow, expert-driven crosswalking process into an afternoon of automated, evidence-backed work.

## The problem
Every growing company eventually faces more than one compliance framework: SOC 2, then ISO 27001, then HIPAA, PCI DSS, CMMC, and more. Each framework states overlapping obligations in its own language. Today, proving that the controls you built for one framework satisfy another is done by hand, by scarce and expensive experts, and it is redone every time a framework is revised. This "crosswalking" step is the painful, unautomated layer that even well-funded compliance tools do not solve.

## The solution
RegMap treats crosswalking as a machine learning retrieval problem. A fine-tuned model maps each control to the equivalent obligations in a target framework, returns a ranked, scored shortlist, and flags low-confidence matches as candidate gaps for an expert to review. The result is a continuous, auditable compliance picture across frameworks, exported as a shareable report. The scanning and evidence layer that surrounds the engine turns live technical findings into compliance evidence.

## Why now, why us
- The category is proven. Vanta, Drata, and Secureframe built large companies on "automate compliance," yet all remain weak at automated cross-framework mapping, which is exactly our wedge.
- We have already built the hard parts: a working mapping engine served as an API, a compliance console that exports audit-ready reports, three released open-source models, reproducible benchmarks, and research manuscripts documenting the method.
- Measured result: fine-tuning lifts top-5 retrieval of the correct provision from 0.500 to 0.735 on held-out data with bootstrap confidence intervals, a large and defensible gain over both generic embeddings and keyword search.

## The wedge and the funnel
We enter as the cross-framework mapping layer for mid-market companies pursuing their second framework, the exact moment the manual pain appears. A companion hands-on training platform (auto-graded labs built on our ground-truth data) is a low-cost acquisition funnel and a trust builder that most compliance startups lack.

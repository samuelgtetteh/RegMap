Public-domain frameworks (NIST, HIPAA, GDPR) ship with full control text. Copyrighted frameworks (ISO, PCI DSS, SOC 2, CIS) are mapped by control ID and paraphrased intent, never verbatim text. HITRUST CSF and COBIT require a licence and stay disabled until one is in place.

Ship it anywhere
